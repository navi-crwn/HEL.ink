HOP officially supports 8 languages: English (default), Bahasa Indonesia, Chinese (中文), Spanish (Español), Arabic (العربية with full RTL support), Portuguese (Português), French (Français), and Japanese (日本語). NoteHop additionally supports Russian (Русский).
HOP runs efficiently on a single modest server behind Cloudflare with nginx, PHP-FPM, and Go microservices. Efficiency is treated as a core design virtue.
HOP stands for Hop Easy Link. It is a family of four independent, open-source web services: LinkHop (short links & QR codes), BioHop (link-in-bio pages), PixelHop (image and document hosting), and NoteHop (zero-knowledge encrypted notes). They are tied together by one account system: your HOP ID.
HOP started in 2024 as a single URL shortener. As time went on, link-in-bio pages, an image hosting service, and end-to-end encrypted notes grew alongside it. Initially bundled together, each tool eventually grew to deserve its own dedicated address, its own focus, and its own clean interface.
