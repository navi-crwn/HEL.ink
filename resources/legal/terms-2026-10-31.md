## 2. Service-Specific Terms

The following provisions apply specifically to individual HOP applications and are legally binding alongside each application's local guidelines.

### 2.1 LinkHop (Short Links & Analytics)
- **Service Address:** `link.hel.ink` (public short links at `hel.ink/l/{slug}`)
- **Link Safety:** All URLs submitted for shortening are screened against automated reputation filters and threat intelligence databases. Any short link found redirecting to phishing, fraud, credential harvesting, or malware will be immediately disabled.
- **Fair Use & Automated Shortening:** Automated link shortening via the API is permitted within published rate limits. Creating thousands of disposable short links for spam campaigns is prohibited.
- **Legacy Slugs:** Short links created prior to namespace isolation retain backwards-compatible redirects under transition policies until formal retirement.

### 2.2 BioHop (Link-in-Bio Profiles)
- **Service Address:** `bio.hel.ink` (public bio pages at `hel.ink/b/{username}`)
- **Content Responsibility:** You are solely responsible for the links, embeds, images, text, and contact information published on your bio page.
- **Sensitive and 18+ Content:** Pages containing mature, adult, or sensitive material must enable the built-in age/content warning gate. Content that depicts illegal acts or child sexual abuse material (CSAM) is strictly forbidden and subject to immediate referral to law enforcement.
- **Custom CSS & Themes:** Custom styling must not hide mandatory safety elements, tamper with security headers, or spoof system authentication dialogues.

### 2.3 PixelHop (Image & File Hosting)
- **Service Address:** `pixel.hel.ink` (shared content at `p.hel.ink/{slug}`)
- **Permitted Formats:** Images, short video clips, and document files (PDF) within configured upload size limits.
- **Metadata Stripping:** For user privacy, PixelHop strips location (GPS) and personal EXIF metadata from uploaded images before public serving.
- **Copyright and DMCA:** You must hold the necessary rights to any content you upload. Verified DMCA copyright infringement notices submitted with proof of ownership will result in prompt file removal.
- **Prohibited Media:** Pirated software, malware droppers, and illegal imagery are strictly forbidden and deleted upon discovery.

### 2.4 NoteHop (Zero-Knowledge Encrypted Notes)
- **Service Address:** `note.hel.ink` (short URLs at `n.hel.ink/{slug}`)
- **Zero-Knowledge Architecture:** NoteHop encrypts all note text and attachments client-side in your browser using AES-GCM before transmission. The decryption key resides exclusively in the browser URL hash fragment (`#k=...`) and is never transmitted to, received by, or stored on the server.
- **Key Custody & Loss:** Because the server possesses zero knowledge of decryption keys, HOP administrators cannot recover, decrypt, or reset lost keys or forgotten note passwords. Lost keys result in permanent, irrecoverable loss of note content.
- **Ephemeral & Burn-after-Reading:** Notes configured to expire or burn after reading are purged automatically from storage upon redemption or expiration.
